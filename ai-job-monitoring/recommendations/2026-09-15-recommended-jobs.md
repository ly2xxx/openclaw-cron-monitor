## Notes on staleness

⚠️ marks roles posted 30+ days ago. The scanner tracks reposts, but a long-refreshed listing often
means the role is filled, the recruiter is farming CVs, or the req is frozen. For these, go around
the listing: find the hiring manager on LinkedIn and approach directly. **A stale listing is a lead,
not an application.**

Live rows span 2026-07-05 → 2026-09-15. Anything older than ~60 days is best treated as a contact
opportunity only.
